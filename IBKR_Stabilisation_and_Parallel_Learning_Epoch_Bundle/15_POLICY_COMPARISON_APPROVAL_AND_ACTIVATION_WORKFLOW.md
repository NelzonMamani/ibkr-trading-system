# 15 — POLICY COMPARISON, APPROVAL, AND ACTIVATION WORKFLOW

## Objective
Ensure policy evolution is controlled and auditable.

## Workflow states
- DRAFT → REVIEWED → (APPROVED | REJECTED)

### DRAFT
Generated by learning engine.

### REVIEWED
A human (you) or a designated approver reviews:
- diff
- rationale
- backtest / replay evidence (if available)

### APPROVED
Approval creates an “active candidate” but does not switch live mode automatically.

### Activation (manual)
Activation is performed by:
- selecting the proposal id
- writing a new policy file/version under strategy policy folder
- updating config to point to it

## Required tooling
- CLI:
  - `learning list-proposals --strategy ROSS_MOMENTUM`
  - `learning show-proposal --id <proposal_id>`
  - `learning approve --id <proposal_id> --by <name>`
  - `learning reject --id <proposal_id> --reason "..."`
- A diff printer that shows field-by-field changes.

## Safety constraints
- Activation must be blocked unless:
  - mandatory verification suite passes
  - a “policy activation ack” env var is present:
    - `POLICY_ACTIVATION_ACK=true`

## Acceptance criteria
- Proposals are stored with audit trail.
- No live code changes occur during proposal generation.
