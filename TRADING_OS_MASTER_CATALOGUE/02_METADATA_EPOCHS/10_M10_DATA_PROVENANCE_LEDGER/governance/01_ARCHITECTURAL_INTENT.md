# M10 — ARCHITECTURAL INTENT

This epoch defines the authoritative record of what data was used, when, in which mode,
from which source, and with what level of trust.

M10 is metadata-only. It does not fetch data, cache data, or block trading.
Its mission is to make data usage auditable, comparable across modes, and trustworthy.

Premarket preparation and on-demand symbol hydration are explicitly within scope:
any preparation that influences signals, decisions, risk, or execution MUST be registered.
