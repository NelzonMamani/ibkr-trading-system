## 1) Scope
This strategy consumes OS capabilities and must not implement OS engines. All canonical IDs are classified and mapped.

## 2) Setup Families (SF_*) scope
ALLOWED:
- SF_MEAN_REVERSION_EXTENSION
- SF_MEAN_REVERSION_FAILURE
- SF_MEAN_REVERSION_BOUNCE
- SF_VWAP_FADE
- SF_KEY_LEVEL_RECLAIM

OPTIONAL:
- SF_OPENING_RANGE_FAKEOUT
- SF_FAILED_BREAKOUT
- SF_FAILED_BREAKDOWN
- SF_LIQUIDITY_SWEEP

DENIED:
- Momentum continuation: SF_MICRO_PULLBACK, SF_FIRST_PULLBACK, SF_SECOND_PULLBACK, SF_BULL_FLAG, SF_TIGHT_FLAG, SF_GAP_AND_GO, etc.
- Event/halt specials: SF_HALT_RESUME, SF_EVENT_* (until specialised)
- Long-horizon: SF_LONG_TERM_*, SF_MACRO_*, SF_WEEKLY_*
