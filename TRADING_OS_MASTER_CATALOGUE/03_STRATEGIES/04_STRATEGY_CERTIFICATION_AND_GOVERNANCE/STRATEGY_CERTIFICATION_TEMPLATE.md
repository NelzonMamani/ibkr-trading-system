# STRATEGY_CERTIFICATION_TEMPLATE

Defines what 'Strategy Complete' means.

Required sections:
1. Identity & Sessions
2. Universe & Eligibility
3. Setup Families
4. Conditions
5. Confirmations
6. Triggers
7. Intrabar Authority (if used)
8. Structural Law (if used)
9. Position Management
10. Trailing Model
11. Exit Model
12. Failure Modes
13. Risk Model
14. Artifact Emission
15. Lifecycle Traceability

A strategy is CERTIFIED only if all required sections exist and pass integrity checks.

END
