# PHASE 4 — FUTURE STRATEGY ADMISSION POLICY

## Objective
Define institutional entry standards for any new strategy (P21+).

## Admission Requirements

Any new strategy must:

- Implement full StrategyPolicyV2 structure.
- Define all domains D0–D14 explicitly.
- Pass institutional audit with no FAIL domains.
- Provide setup taxonomy, trigger model, risk governance, exit doctrine, execution constraints, and failure modes.

## Rejection Conditions

Strategy is rejected if:
- Default-only declarations are present.
- Domains are missing.
- Execution doctrine undefined.
- Risk model ambiguous.

New strategies begin as CONDITIONALLY_CERTIFIED until full audit pass.
