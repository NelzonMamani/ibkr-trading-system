## Trading OS Certification Programme — Law of Operation

This trading system is treated as live, functional reality.

Epochs are applied to the current state of the system, not to recreate history.

Every epoch must be processed and end in certification.

Rules:
- Epochs are certified against existing reality.
- Missing parts are added if they improve the current system.
- Weak invariants are tightened.
- Improvements are additive and monotonic.
- Tradeability must never be reduced.
- Duplication is forbidden; one authoritative implementation must exist.
- Redundant or unused components must be removed under housekeeping governance.
- No epoch may be skipped.
- No epoch may perform a clean-slate rewrite.
- No large subsystem may be rewritten without explicit instruction.
- Capability must never be removed to satisfy documentation.

Primary purpose:
To trade efficiently, safely, and to grow capital.
