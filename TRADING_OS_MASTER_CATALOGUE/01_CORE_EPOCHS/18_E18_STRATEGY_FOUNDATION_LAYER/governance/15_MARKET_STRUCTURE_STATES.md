# E18 — MARKET STRUCTURE STATES (NEUTRAL DESCRIPTORS)

Market structure states are shared descriptors.
They are NOT strategy policy and NOT regime classification (which may be elsewhere).

CHECKLIST (minimum vocabulary):
[ ] STRUCTURE_TREND_UP (HH/HL)
[ ] STRUCTURE_TREND_DOWN (LH/LL)
[ ] STRUCTURE_BALANCED (range/balance)
[ ] STRUCTURE_COMPRESSION (contraction)
[ ] STRUCTURE_EXPANSION (expansion)
[ ] STRUCTURE_BREAK (key structural break)
[ ] STRUCTURE_RECLAIM (structural reclaim)
[ ] STRUCTURE_FAILURE (failed break / failed reclaim)
[ ] FOLLOW_THROUGH_PRESENT (yes/no descriptor)
[ ] CHOP_STATE (yes/no descriptor)

INVARIANT:
Structure states must be derived deterministically from defined rules, and exposed as inputs.

END
