# E17_STRATEGY_INTERACTION_RULES — PROBLEM STATEMENT

When multiple strategies are enabled simultaneously, failures emerge:

- Competing entries on the same symbol
- Opposing positions opened concurrently
- Capital over-allocation
- Conflicting exits and stops
- One strategy invalidating another silently

These are not strategy bugs.
They are SYSTEM-level coordination failures.

E17 exists to solve these problems explicitly.
