# E17_STRATEGY_INTERACTION_RULES — OVERVIEW

E17 defines how multiple strategies may coexist safely inside the Trading OS.

This epoch governs:
- Strategy coexistence
- Cross-strategy interference prevention
- Priority and conflict resolution
- Capital and authority isolation

Without E17, strategies behave correctly in isolation but can
interfere destructively when combined.

E17 ensures the Trading OS behaves as a single coherent trader,
not a collection of competing bots.
