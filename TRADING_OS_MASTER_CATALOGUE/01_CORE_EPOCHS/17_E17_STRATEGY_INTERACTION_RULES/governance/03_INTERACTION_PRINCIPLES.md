# E17_STRATEGY_INTERACTION_RULES — PRINCIPLES

Principle 1: Strategy Isolation
Each strategy owns its decisions but not global authority.

Principle 2: Explicit Interaction
No strategy may affect another implicitly.

Principle 3: Capital Coherence
Capital is allocated centrally, not per strategy.

Principle 4: Deterministic Resolution
Conflicts must resolve deterministically.

Principle 5: Transparency
All interaction outcomes must be auditable.

END
