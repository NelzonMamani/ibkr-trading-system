# Long Horizon Value Strategy (Buffett-Style)

## Purpose
This strategy identifies, evaluates, and allocates capital to high-quality businesses
with durable economic moats, conservative financial structures, and a meaningful
margin of safety relative to intrinsic value.

This is a **long-horizon, fundamental strategy**. It is intentionally slow, patient,
and capital-aware. It runs **after-hours / weekends only** and produces **trade intents**
— never direct execution.

## Core Principles (Non-Negotiable)
- Circle of Competence: invest only in understandable businesses.
- Economic Moat: durable competitive advantage is required.
- Financial Strength: avoid permanent capital loss.
- Owner’s Earnings: focus on true economic earnings, not optics.
- Margin of Safety: price must allow for error.
- Capital Awareness: YES does not always mean BUY NOW.
- Global First: no market is excluded due to ignorance.
- Priority, Not Exclusion: unfamiliarity increases conservatism, not rejection.

## Decision States
- NEVER: banned or structurally unacceptable.
- NO: fails business or economic logic.
- WATCHLIST: great business, wrong price or incomplete.
- FOCUS: buy-worthy, capital constrained or awaiting approval.
- BUY: executable (subject to risk & capital).

## Execution Doctrine
- This strategy never bypasses Risk.
- Orders are expressed as percentage-based intents.
- Manual or automatic execution is configurable.

## Anti-Goals
- No intraday trading.
- No technical indicators.
- No market timing.
- No forced diversification.
