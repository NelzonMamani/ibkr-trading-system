# Failure Modes & Safeties

## Failure modes
- Fading without proof (no exhaustion)
- Fighting trend days
- Chasing entries
- Loose or moving stops
- Hope-based holds
- Illiquid/wide spread symbols

## Safeties
- Safe-by-default (no trade unless gates pass)
- Structural stop required
- Stop width cap
- Minimum R:R required
- Regime veto early
